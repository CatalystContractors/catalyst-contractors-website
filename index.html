Who We Are

One crew. More capability.

Catalyst Contractors LLC is a locally owned Ashland-area construction company with broad hands-on capability across concrete, remodeling, carpentry, framing and property improvement.

Concrete is a major focus of the business, but the same skilled crew also builds decks and porches, remodels kitchens and bathrooms, installs flooring and tongue-and-groove walls and ceilings, builds shutters and mailboxes, constructs decorative retaining walls and flowerbeds, and handles many of the projects that make a home or property better.
